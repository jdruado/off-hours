Privacy Policy

Off/Hours respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, you send us an email. That email may include your name, email address, delivery address, phone number, and order details. We only collect what you choose to send us.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Process and fulfill your order
  • Reply to your inquiries
  • Contact you about your order

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell or share your personal information. We only share it with trusted third parties when necessary to process your order or when the law requires it.

4. Keeping your information

We keep order emails only as long as necessary to fulfill your order and for record-keeping purposes. We delete personal information when it is no longer needed.

5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you. Please contact us through our Contact page for any requests regarding your personal information.

6. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy at any time. Using the website after changes have been made means you accept the updated policy. The latest version of this policy will always be available on this page. We recommend checking this page regularly for updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, feel free to reach out to our team through our Contact page.
