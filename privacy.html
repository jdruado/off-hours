— LEGAL / POLICY

Privacy Policy.

At Off/Hours, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us when visiting our online store or purchasing our minimalist apparel.

1. Information We Collect

Our website functions as a static storefront. We do not automatically store your personal data in background databases when you browse. We only receive personal information that you intentionally provide when submitting an inquiry or placing an order via email (such as your name, delivery address, and selected t-shirt size and quantity).

2. How We Use Your Information

The details you provide through our ordering or contact mechanisms are used strictly to fulfill your t-shirt orders, respond to your inquiries, and manage customer service communications. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party marketers.

3. Email Communications & Ordering

When you click the order button on our product pages, your device's email client opens with a pre-filled message sent directly to our store. Because communications are handled through your personal email provider, your messages are also governed by their respective privacy terms and security protocols.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, feel free to reach out to our team through our Contact page.
